# Code of Conduct

## NEXARIUM Community Principles

NEXARIUM is an open decentralized infrastructure environment focused on semantic systems, identity continuity, interoperability, institutional transparency, and long-term digital persistence.

Participation within the ecosystem should remain respectful, constructive, and compatible with the principles of open decentralized infrastructure.

---

# Core Principles

## Respect

All participants, contributors, researchers, developers, and community members must be treated with respect regardless of background, identity, nationality, or technical perspective.

## Transparency

Open communication, verifiable information, and institutional integrity are encouraged across all public interactions connected to the ecosystem.

## Interoperability

NEXARIUM supports open standards, semantic compatibility, federated communication, and decentralized collaboration across different technological environments.

## Constructive Contribution

Contributions should support the long-term stability, integrity, readability, and continuity of the infrastructure and associated documentation.

## Decentralized Ethics

The ecosystem encourages decentralized and open technological development while rejecting malicious behavior, disinformation, harassment, abuse, and destructive activity.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Hate speech or abusive language
- Malicious infrastructure attacks
- Intentional misinformation
- Spam or fraudulent activity
- Distribution of harmful software
- Attempts to compromise infrastructure integrity
- Disruptive or destructive conduct

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories
- Infrastructure discussions
- Public ecosystem communication
- Federated communication environments
- Documentation contributions
- Associated digital environments connected to NEXARIUM

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to moderate, remove, restrict, or reject contributions or interactions that violate these principles or threaten the integrity of the ecosystem.
